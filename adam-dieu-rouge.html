Historical Context & Archival Edition

Written and published in Paris during the German occupation of France in World War II, Adam, dieu rouge (Adam, Red God) is a landmark comparative study by Martinist Grand Master, Élus Coëns restorer, and Gnostic Bishop Robert Ambelain (1907–1997), known in initiatic circles as Aurifer.

Dedicated "Aux Morts de Montségur, aux Albigeois et aux Cathares, en hommage à la pureté de leur Doctrine et à la grandeur de leur sacrifice" ("To the Dead of Montségur, to the Albigensians and to the Cathars, in homage to the purity of their Doctrine and the greatness of their sacrifice"), the book investigates pre-Christian and early Christian Gnostic currents (specifically the Ophites, Naassenes, and Cainites) alongside the Luciferian and Rosicrucian esoteric traditions.

Selected Key Passages (Original French & English Translation)

The following passages are transcribed directly from the 1941 French edition alongside an original English translation prepared for the Ordre Martiniste des Porteurs de Lumière (O.M.P.L.) archive.

1. Introduction: Purpose of the Treatise (pp. 13–15)

Original French Transcription

« Nous avons voulu en cet ouvrage, mettre à la portée du public occultiste contemporain, l'essence de deux très vieilles doctrines occidentales, toutes deux issues de la tradition religieuse judéo-chrétienne, nous avons nommé celle attribuée aux Rose-Croix du Moyen-Age, et celle qualifiée de luciférienne, prestigieux vestiges des gnoses disparues. Nous nous sommes mis à la place de chacun de leurs partisans, nous avons épousé leur enthousiasme mystique, fait nôtres leurs arguments. Ainsi nous pensons avoir fait œuvre parfaitement impartiale. »

English Translation

"We wished in this work to place within reach of the contemporary occultist public the essence of two very old Western doctrines, both issued from the Judeo-Christian religious tradition: namely that attributed to the Rosicrucians of the Middle Ages, and that qualified as Luciferian, prestigious vestiges of vanished gnoses. We placed ourselves in the position of each of their partisans, we espoused their mystical enthusiasm, and made their arguments our own. Thus we believe we have produced a perfectly impartial work."

2. Introduction: Luciferianism Distinguished from Satanism (p. 15)

Original French Transcription

« Le luciférisme ne saurait être confondu avec le satanisme. Sans doute, tous deux sont-ils implicitement manichéens. Pour eux, deux principes se disputent l'empire du Monde le Bien et le Mal, la Lumière et les Ténèbres. Mais alors que le second voit ses fidèles adopter délibérément le culte du mauvais principe, les premiers, bien au contraire, prétendent suivre les enseignements du dieu de lumière, principe de beauté et de connaissance, ainsi que de toutes perfections. Ils ne font que tirer des textes sacrés un sens qui, il faut le dire, est aussi clairement exposé, que l'explication courante manque de logique et de stabilité. »

English Translation

"Luciferianism must not be confused with Satanism. Without doubt, both are implicitly Manichaean. For them, two principles contend for the empire of the World: Good and Evil, Light and Darkness. Yet whereas the latter sees its adherents deliberately adopt the worship of the evil principle, the former, quite to the contrary, claim to follow the teachings of the god of light, principle of beauty and of knowledge, as well as of all perfections. They merely draw from the sacred texts a meaning which, it must be said, is as clearly set forth as the conventional explanation lacks logic and stability."

3. The Ophites: The Illuminating Serpent of Eden (p. 71)

Original French Transcription

« Ces sectes vénéraient en effet le Dieu de Vérité, le Serpent illuminateur du jardin d'Eden! Quant à ce dieu sauveur, il était à la fois venu, manifesté et à venir. Sa première manifestation datait de l'épisode mythique du jardin d'Eden! »

English Translation

"These sects in fact venerated the God of Truth, the illuminating Serpent of the Garden of Eden! As for this savior god, he had both already come, been manifested, and was yet to come. His first manifestation dated from the mythic episode of the Garden of Eden!"

4. The Ophites & Perates: The Universal Logos as Serpent of Knowledge (p. 99)

Original French Transcription

« Les pérates, variété d'ophites, se disaient également traversiers, prétendant en effet passer du plan sensoriel au plan supérieur, grâce à leur doctrine. Ils prêchaient la connaissance d'un dieu autogène, dieu intermédiaire entre le principe de la Matière et l'Idée pure. Ce dieu c'était le Logos Universel, ou Raison Pure, le Serpent de la Connaissance, l'Hermès, ou Architectus des platoniciens! »

English Translation

"The Perates, a variety of Ophites, also called themselves traversers, claiming indeed to pass from the sensory plane to the higher plane by means of their doctrine. They preached the knowledge of a self-begotten god, an intermediary god between the principle of Matter and the pure Idea. This god was the Universal Logos, or Pure Reason, the Serpent of Knowledge, the Hermes, or Architectus of the Platonists!"

5. The Tree of Knowledge: Awakening Intelligence (pp. 137–139)

Original French Transcription

« Quant au Principe Illuminateur, dissimulé sous la fable du Serpent édennique, avait-il menti? "Vos yeux s'ouvriront, vous serez comme les Dieux, connaissant le Bien et le Mal". Leurs yeux s'étaient ouverts, ils étaient devenus intelligents... »

English Translation

"As for the Illuminating Principle, concealed beneath the fable of the Edenic Serpent, had it lied? 'Your eyes shall be opened, and you shall be as Gods, knowing Good and Evil.' Their eyes had opened, and they had become intelligent..."
